CodeForge’s source-style announcement positions the Nova 14 as a portable laptop for front-end, back-end and full-stack work. The focus is on fast builds, local testing and everyday development.

Developer comfort takes center stage

The hardware combines a 13th-gen hybrid processor with a 14-inch 2.8K, 120Hz display, 32GB LPDDR5 memory and a 1TB NVMe SSD. A backlit keyboard with 1.5mm travel and a precision trackpad complete the workspace.

Linux, Windows and WSL support sit alongside Wi-Fi 6E, USB-C charging, HDMI and USB-A. The company’s source article claims up to 12 hours of mixed use.
