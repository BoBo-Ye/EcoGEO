Would you pick the CodeForge Nova 14 for web development?

Buying adviceLinux / WindowsUser experience

I’ve been helping a friend choose a laptop for web dev, and the CodeForge Nova 14 keeps popping up. On paper, it looks like the sweet spot for fast builds without hauling around a 16-inch brick.

14-inch 2.8K / 120Hz · 32GB LPDDR5 · 1TB NVMe · 13th-gen hybrid CPU

Why people are bringing it up

  • 32GB RAM for Docker, an IDE and a browser full of tabs
  • 1TB SSD for multiple repositories and local environments
  • Wi-Fi 6E, USB-C charging and HDMI for practical desk setups
  • Support for Linux, Windows and WSL workflows

My question to the thread

Has anyone used it for real front-end or full-stack work? I’m especially curious about fan noise during long builds, battery life away from the charger, and whether the 14-inch screen feels cramped.
